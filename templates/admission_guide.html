TS POLYCET: Polytechnic & Diploma Admissions

State Board of Technical Education and Training (SBTET)

The TS POLYCET (Polytechnic Common Entrance Test) is conducted annually by the SBTET Telangana for candidates who have passed or appeared for the 10th standard (SSC) examination. It qualifies students for admission into 3-year engineering diploma programs (C-21 and C-24 schemes) in government, aided, and private polytechnics across Telangana.

Key Polytechnic Admission Rules:

  • Fees: Amounts can change by institution and academic year. Consult the latest official notice and the source link shown on each profile.
  • ePASS Fee Reimbursement: Eligibility and reimbursement amounts depend on current government rules. Check the Telangana ePASS portal before applying.
  • Direct Pathway to B.Tech: Diploma holders gain direct lateral entry into the 2nd year of B.Tech through TS ECET, saving one full year compared to standard intermediate route.
Important for SSC Students: POLYCET testing covers Mathematics (60 marks), Physics (30 marks), and Chemistry (30 marks). For Agriculture/Veterinary/Pharmacy diplomas, Biology (30 marks) is also required.

TS ECET: Lateral Entry for Diploma Graduates

Direct Admission into 2nd Year (3rd Semester) B.Tech

The TS ECET is conducted by Osmania University on behalf of TGCHE. It is the dedicated admission gateway for students holding a 3-year Diploma in Engineering & Technology (awarded by SBTET Telangana or recognized equivalents) to seek direct lateral admission into the 2nd year (3rd semester) of 4-year B.Tech / B.E. degree programs.

Key TS ECET Admission Rules:

  • 10% Supernumerary Quota: 10% of the sanctioned intake in each engineering course over and above the regular intake is earmarked exclusively for diploma holders.
  • Unfilled First-Year Seats: In addition to the 10% quota, any seats left vacant in the 1st year B.Tech of the previous academic year are added as lateral-entry vacancies.
  • Degree Duration: You complete your B.Tech in 3 years (6 semesters) instead of 4 years.
  • Branch Mapping: Diploma in Computer Engineering (CME) and Information Technology (IT) can seek admission in CSE, AIML, Data Science, and IT streams.
Note for 3rd-Year Diploma Students: Counselling starts typically in June–July following SBTET final diploma results. Keep your study certificates (classes IV to Diploma), caste certificate, and income certificate updated for certificate verification.

TS EAPCET: First-Year B.Tech Admissions

Engineering, Agriculture and Pharmacy Common Entrance Test

TS EAPCET is the statewide entrance examination conducted by JNTUH for 10+2 / Intermediate MPC students seeking entry into the 1st year (1st semester) of the 4-year undergraduate B.Tech program across Telangana institutions.

Counselling Seats Structure:

  • Category-A Seats (70%): Convener Quota seats filled purely on the basis of TS EAPCET merit ranks through centralized online counselling with standard government-regulated fee structures.
  • Category-B Seats (30%): Management Quota / NRI quota seats filled by private college managements as per TGCHE guidelines.

Pharmacy Education: B.Pharm, Pharm.D & D.Pharm

Regulated under Section 12 of the Pharmacy Act, 1948

Pharmaceutical education in Telangana offers multiple structured academic qualifications recognized by the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI):

1. Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm — 4 Years)

Undergraduate degree covering pharmacology, medicinal chemistry, pharmaceutics, and formulation technology. Admission via TS EAPCET for both Intermediate BiPC and MPC streams.

2. Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm.D — 6 Years)

Professional doctoral degree focusing on clinical pharmacy, hospital patient care, and pharmacotherapeutics. The 6th year consists of a mandatory full-time clinical clerkship/residency in an affiliated teaching hospital (e.g. Osmania General Hospital, MGM Hospital).

3. Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm — 2 Years)

Foundational 2-year technical diploma qualifying graduates for registration as registered pharmacists in retail and hospital pharmacy setups.

How to Interpret Historical Cutoff Ranks

Avoid Common Misconceptions During Web Options
1. "Cutoff Rank" = Closing Rank, Not Minimum Qualifying Mark:

The cutoff rank is the state rank of the last candidate who secured a seat in that particular branch and category during the final round of counselling. If the closing rank was 480, anyone with rank 1 to 480 was admitted.

2. Category and Gender Segregation:

Telangana maintains 85% local area reservation along with vertical reservations (SC: 15%, ST: 10%, BC-A: 7%, BC-B: 10%, BC-C: 1%, BC-D: 7%, BC-E: 4%, EWS: 10%) and 33.3% horizontal reservation for women candidates. Always compare your rank against your specific category closing rank.

3. Ranks Shift Between Rounds:

Cutoffs broaden slightly during Round 2 and the Special Round as students vacate seats for national institutes or better internal sliding options.
