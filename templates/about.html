About College Darpan

Your Future. Your College. Your Choice.

The Origin & Vision

As a third-year Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIML) diploma student in Telangana, I experienced firsthand the confusion and misinformation surrounding higher education admissions: commercial portals flooded with sponsored banners, hidden tuition fee extras, and zero transparent information for diploma students preparing for TS ECET lateral-entry into 2nd year B.Tech.

College Darpan helps students explore Polytechnic / Diploma, B.Tech / Engineering, and Pharmacy records in Telangana. Newly added SBTET directory entries include a source and academic year; older records and programme, fee, cutoff, and placement details still need field-by-field rechecking.

AI Recommendation Methodology

Instead of an opaque "black-box" model that hallucinates cutoffs or packages, College Darpan implements an explainable Hybrid Multi-Criteria Recommendation Architecture:

1. Content-Based Multi-Category Matching

Evaluates direct branch matches (100% for direct matches like AIML or CME, 75% for closely allied disciplines) and district geographic convenience.

2. Rule-Based Eligibility Safeguards

Compares applicant rank against verified historical closing ranks for TS POLYCET, TS EAPCET, and TS ECET. If data is unverified, it explicitly shows: "Admission eligibility cannot be confirmed with the available data."

3. Normalized Multi-Criteria Weighted Scoring

Calculates the overall score ($0–100$) using student-customized priority weights normalized strictly to $1.0$:

Score = w_branch(S_branch) + w_fee(S_fee) + w_placement(S_placement) + w_location(S_loc) + w_infra(S_infra)
