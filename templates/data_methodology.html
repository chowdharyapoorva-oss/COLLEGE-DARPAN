What the current verification covers

Institution listing is separate from programme and outcome verification

Commercial educational portals frequently display exaggerated placement statistics, unverified packages, or fabricated cutoff ranks to promote private advertisers.

New SBTET directory entries include a direct source URL, academic year, and a verification status for the institution listing. This does not verify fees, programmes, cutoffs, placements, or other metrics. Existing imported records keep their previous status and still need a field-by-field review. Missing data should be treated as unavailable.

Primary Government & Regulatory Sources

Where our institutional records originate
1. Telangana Admission and Fee Regulatory Committee (TAFRC) Not audited in this update

TAFRC is a primary source for regulated fee notices. This update did not import or re-verify fee values. Check the current notice for the institution and academic year before relying on a fee figure. Open TAFRC.

2. State Board of Technical Education & Training (SBTET Telangana) Institution names, 2026–27

The added directory names are listed in the SBTET LPCET 2026–27 booklet. We did not infer programme availability or intake from these names. Open the official booklet.

3. Telangana State Council of Higher Education (TGCHE) Current-year entries pending

TGCHE counselling sources can support current institute and cutoff research. Existing cutoff rows have not been rechecked for this update. Open the TGEAPCET institute profile.

4. Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) Statutory Pharmacy Approvals

Every pharmacy institution's approval status under Section 12 of the Pharmacy Act 1948 is checked against the central PCI list for B.Pharm, Pharm.D (6-Year Clinical Doctorate), and D.Pharm offerings.

Transparent Scoring Formulation

College Darpan computes match scores ($0–100$) using linear normalized combinations of student-weighted dimensions:

Score = w_branch(S_branch) + w_fee(S_fee) + w_placement(S_placement) + w_location(S_loc) + w_infra(S_infra)

Where weights satisfy $\sum w_i = 1.0$. If a college is missing official fee or placement records, the engine applies a conservative penalty rather than disqualifying the college, and generates an explicit textual caution explaining the exact data uncertainty.
